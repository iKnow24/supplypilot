# In-store demo (2 minutes, on your phone)

The goal: a bakery, café or restaurant owner watches for two minutes and thinks "we could use
this tomorrow." Show their world, not features. Everything below is on
https://supplypilot-nu.vercel.app/home.

## Before you walk in (30 seconds)

1. Pick the demo business that matches the store with the selector at the top:
   - **Bakery** → Sunday Crumb Bakery
   - **Café / coffee shop** → Third Shift Coffee
   - **Restaurant** → Little Juniper
2. Open **Home** once, so Autopilot has the order ready before you hand them the phone.
3. Have a vendor price text or invoice photo ready on your phone, in case they don't show you one.

## The talk track

**Open with their pain, not the app (15 sec).**
> "Quick question: how many vendors do you order from, and how long does ordering take you each
> week?" Listen, and write down their numbers.

**1. Home: it already did the work (30 sec).**
> "This is a bakery like yours. I didn't press anything. It saw the flour runs out before
> Friday's delivery, drafted Friday's order, and priced it across every vendor: $1,467.79, one
> delivery. You just approve."

Point at **Needs attention** (red) and **Your next order** (the price, and which vendors).

**2. Tap the red item: who's cheapest right now (20 sec).**
> "Flour's the problem. Here's every vendor's price per pound, with your own deals applied, and
> when each one can deliver."

**3. Stock: the whole pantry at a glance (15 sec).**
> "Every item, how many days it lasts, and the dashed line is the delivery. Anything red runs
> out before the truck comes."

**4. Their own prices, live (30 sec). This is the "wow."**
**Orders → Vendors → Add prices from a screenshot.**
> "Got a text from your dairy rep, or an invoice? Let's snap it." Upload it, and the prices
> appear for them to approve. "Now it compares *your* real prices."

**5. Approve (10 sec).**
Back on **Home**, tap **Approve**: a draft purchase order and a PDF.
> "Nothing is ever sent to a supplier without you."

**The close (the actual goal).**
> "Can we set this up with your last few invoices this week? It's free for 30 days. If it
> doesn't save you time or money, you've lost nothing."

The win is a yes, plus photos of 2–3 recent invoices.

## Questions they'll ask

| They say | You say |
|---|---|
| "I already have a rep I like." | "Keep them. It shows you when their price is the best and when it isn't, and their minimums and cutoffs." |
| "Does it place orders for me?" | "It drafts and prices them. You approve, and it makes the purchase order. Nothing goes out without you." |
| "How do you know my stock?" | "A quick weekly shelf count to start. Later it can come from your register sales." |
| "What does it cost?" | "Free for 30 days while we set it up together." (Decide pricing before this comes up twice.) |
| "Is this real?" | "The vendors in this demo are pretend. With you it runs on your real invoices and vendors." |

## After the visit

Within 24 hours, send a thank-you text with what they said their weekly ordering time and vendor
count were. Book the setup visit.
